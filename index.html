Why I do this work

To Whom It May Concern,

I am a sophomore at Rutgers University–New Brunswick majoring in Computer Science with a minor in Business Administration, and I am writing to share my interest in internship and early-career opportunities in data, technology, and healthcare. I am passionate about using technology and data to solve real-world problems and to improve people's lives, and I am especially drawn to the place where technology, business, and healthcare meet.

As a Data and IT Intern with the H.U.G. Reading Program, I work with student progress data and technology systems. I clean and validate program records and built an Amazon QuickSight dashboard that helps staff track attendance. My tutor-retention analysis led to a feedback-survey recommendation that the program is now putting into practice. That experience showed me that the best analysis is the kind people actually act on.

My projects connect directly to my goals. I helped build Sleep Doctor, a machine learning project that predicts sleep quality using Python and Streamlit, and I created Tableau and Power BI dashboards that turn raw data into clear insights. With a team, I also designed HealthMate, an accessible health-app concept for older adults that won Best UI/UX Design among 50+ teams at the UHSACCS Hackathon. As a Precalculus SI Leader and Adobe Student Ambassador, I have also learned to explain complex ideas clearly to different audiences.

Ultimately, I want to combine my technical and business skills with my passion for helping people and making a meaningful difference. I would welcome the chance to bring that mix of data skills, design sense, and communication to a team that shares this goal. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
Shaili Halani
